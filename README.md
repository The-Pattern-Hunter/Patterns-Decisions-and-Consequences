## About this book

*Patterns, Decisions, and Consequences* is a fifteen-chapter textbook written for
the Value-Added Course on Artificial Intelligence introduced under India's
National Education Policy (NEP) 2020. It requires no mathematics and no prior
programming knowledge — only the willingness to observe carefully and think
clearly.

The book is organised around a single idea, introduced in Chapter 1 and
returned to throughout: intelligence, human or artificial, is the
**Pattern-Decision-Action loop** — observe a regularity, decide what it
implies, act on it, and learn from the consequences. Every subsequent chapter,
from neural networks to algorithmic bias to AI governance in India, is a
variation on that loop.

Every example in the book is grounded in a specific Indian context — a
fisherwoman reading the Chilika Lake, a doctor in a Chhattisgarh district
hospital, an earthworm genomics lab in Western Odisha — rather than treated as
an abstract, placeless universal.

## Structure

| Unit | Theme | Chapters |
|------|-------|----------|
| 1 | What Is Intelligence? | 1–3 |
| 2 | How Machines Learn | 4–7 |
| 3 | AI in the World | 8–10 |
| 4 | The Consequences of Intelligence | 11–14 |
| 5 | Making Something | 15 |

Each unit opens with a short reflective divider page framing that unit's
question before its chapters begin. The book also includes a Preface, an
Epilogue, a Glossary of key terms, and an Index.
