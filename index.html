Artificial Intelligence for Indian undergraduate students — a fifteen-chapter course built around one idea: intelligence, human or artificial, is a loop of observing, deciding, acting, and learning.

Read the PDF →

Pattern recognition is the oldest form of intelligence. A student reading a friend's mood from a one-word WhatsApp reply, a child reading a parent's tone before asking for pocket money, and a farmer reading the approaching monsoon are all doing what neural networks do: finding structure in signals and acting on it.

This book covers how machines learn, how AI is reshaping human behaviour and society at large, and the ethical questions that follow — grounded throughout in Indian examples, aligned with the Odisha university AI curriculum under NEP 2020. No mathematics required, and no prior programming knowledge assumed. Only the willingness to observe carefully and think clearly.

About the Author
Alok Patel

A researcher with a Ph.D. in Zoology from Utkal University, he has made significant contributions to the development of DNA SNP chips and molecular genetic maps in fishes, with publications in Journal of Genetics, Animal Genetics, and Molecular Ecology Resources. He previously held a tenure-track faculty position at the University of Chile, Santiago, and brings more than fifteen years of teaching experience to the classroom. He serves as a peer reviewer for Molecular Ecology Resources, Molecular Biology Reports, and Reviews in Medical Virology. He is also the author of The Pattern Hunters: The Art of Scientific Thinking and Pattern and Descent: A Textbook of Taxonomy and Evolutionary Biology.

Member, Board of Studies in Zoology, Sambalpur University  ·  Principal Investigator, OSHEC-funded project on earthworm genomics and soil health biomarkers, Western Odisha
